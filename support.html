Restore my subscription

Open Bloom Plus in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel Bloom Plus

In the app: You › All settings › Subscription › Manage subscription. Or in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Reminders don't arrive

Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Bloom, and that reminders are on in You › All settings.

Moving to a new phone

Your progress is kept only on your phone, so it doesn't move with you. Your subscription does: use Restore purchases.
