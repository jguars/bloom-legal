Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Bloom: Healthy Together ("Bloom") is made by ONKI Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your name, your cat's name, your goals, weight entries, habits, moves, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that receive this data, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Bloom Plus, payment is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for Bloom and basic device information (such as device model, operating system and country). It does not receive your name, email address, weight or any of your progress.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device at the times you choose. You can turn them off in the app or in your iPhone's Settings.

No tracking, no ads

Bloom has no advertising and no analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Health information

Bloom is for general wellness. It is not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or weight-loss plan.

Children

Bloom is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: arslanovichg@gmail.com.
