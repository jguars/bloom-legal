Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Bloom: Healthy Together ("Bloom") is made by ONKI Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your name, your cat's name, your goals, weight entries, habits, moves, steps, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have accounts and we can't see this data. Deleting the app deletes it. The only things that leave your phone are described below: purchases, anonymous usage events, and what you choose to share with friends.

Steps and Apple Health

With your permission, Bloom reads the steps your iPhone counts (Motion & Fitness) and, if you turn on Apple Health, your steps from Health and Bloom's moves written there as workouts. Step counts are used on your device to show your day and give paws; they are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn this off in the app or in your iPhone's Settings and the Health app.

Friends

If you invite a friend or join with a friend's code, your cat's name, your cat's look, the decor in your living room and how many active days your journey has are shared through our invite server, with a random code for your phone, so your friends can visit your house and send you a paw. Your name, email, weight, habits and walked steps are not shared, and nothing is shared unless you use an invite.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Bloom Plus or buy an outfit, a pet or a Signature piece, payment is handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for Bloom and basic device information (such as device model, operating system and country). It does not receive your name, email address, weight or any of your progress.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device at the times you choose. You can turn them off in the app or in your iPhone's Settings.

Usage analytics

To learn which parts of Bloom help people keep going, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (hosted in the EU): for example that onboarding finished, a move was done or the paywall was shown, with the day of your journey and whether you have Bloom Plus. Events carry a random identifier made by the app, not your name, email, weight, habits or steps, and they are not linked to you. We don't build profiles and don't record your screen. As with any internet request, PostHog receives your device's IP address; Bloom asks it not to work out your location from it, and we don't use it to identify you.

No tracking, no ads

Bloom has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Health information

Bloom is for general wellness. It is not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or weight-loss plan.

Children

Bloom is not directed at children under 13.
